MAC-LIMPO

Free, open-source disk cleaner and disk space analyzer for macOS

Free up disk space on your Mac. A native menu bar app for developers that cleans Xcode DerivedData, iOS simulators, Docker, node_modules, Rust target/ folders and 40+ caches — and a Disk X-Ray that shows where every gigabyte goes. A free alternative to CleanMyMac and DaisyDisk. No telemetry, no account, no subscription.

Developer caches

Xcode, simulators, Docker, node_modules, Rust, Cargo, Go, pnpm, Bun, npm, pip, NuGet, Android SDK, .NET SDKs, IDE caches and more.

Disk X-Ray

Reads millions of files in seconds. Treemap by file type, folder tree, largest files — pinch to magnify down to a single file, in 2D or 3D.

Safe by default

Scan first, confirm before cleaning, Trash instead of delete where possible, caches only — never your documents.
